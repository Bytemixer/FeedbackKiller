Feedback Resonance Killer v0.4.1 - Windows x64 (prebuilt binaries)
=================================================================

VST3:
  Copy the "Feedback Resonance Killer.vst3" folder into your VST3 directory,
  typically:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

STANDALONE:
  Run "Feedback Resonance Killer.exe" directly (no DAW required).

See MANUAL.md (repository root) for full documentation.
